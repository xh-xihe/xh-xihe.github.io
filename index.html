👋About简介

I design quantum algorithms for machine-learning problems, and use machine learning to find structure inside quantum systems — most recently, to discover and formally certify quantum error-correcting codes. 我设计面向机器学习问题的量子算法，同时用机器学习去挖掘量子系统内部的结构——最近的工作是发现并形式化验证量子纠错码。

I am a Research Associate in the Department of Physics at The University of Texas at Dallas, working with Prof. Bei Zeng. Before Dallas I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (2022–2025) with Prof. Renbao Liu, and I received my Ph.D. in Physics from UESTC in 2021 under Prof. Xiaoting Wang. 我在德克萨斯大学达拉斯分校物理系任助理研究员，合作导师为 曾蓓教授。此前于 2022–2025 年在香港中文大学物理系从事博士后研究，合作导师为刘仁保教授；2021 年在电子科技大学获物理学博士学位，导师为王晓霆教授。

I am always glad to hear from people working on quantum machine learning, quantum error correction, or data-driven approaches to quantum systems — get in touch. 欢迎在量子机器学习、量子纠错以及量子系统的数据驱动方法等方向交流合作——欢迎来信。

🔬Research研究方向

  1. 01

    Quantum Machine Learning量子机器学习

    Machine-learning algorithms built for quantum devices, designed so that the advantage over the classical algorithm is provable or measurable — not assumed. 面向量子设备设计机器学习算法，使其相对经典算法的优势可被证明或被实测，而非仅停留于假设。

  2. 02

    AI for Quantum Error Correction人工智能驱动的量子纠错

    Using multi-agent systems, symbolic search and formal verification to discover quantum codes — and to certify, rather than merely claim, that they work. 用多智能体系统、符号搜索与形式化验证来发现量子码，并对其正确性给出机器可检验的证明，而不只是声称。

  3. 03

    Deep-Learning-Enhanced Quantum Information Extraction深度学习增强的量子信息提取

    Neural networks that learn the evolution and observable data of complex quantum systems, recovering signal that conventional estimators leave behind. 用神经网络学习复杂量子系统的演化与可观测数据，提取传统估计方法难以获取的信号。

  4. 04

    Quantum Transfer & Cross-Domain Learning量子迁移与跨域学习

    Transfer learning, domain adaptation and reinforcement learning recast as quantum problems — aligning distributions, subspaces and correlations on quantum hardware. 将迁移学习、域自适应与强化学习问题重构为量子问题——在量子硬件上对齐分布、子空间与相关性。
